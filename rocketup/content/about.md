Hi, I'm Giuseppe Della Vedova, a computer scientist from Italy.

I'm mostly focused on ML, AI and distributed computing (+ in the spare time I work on interpreters and text-based games), with a shared focus on optimized code.

I develop in Rust, Python, JS/TS and C; I'm currently learning Haskell.

I don't often work on frontend, but when I do I use [Svelte](https://svelte.dev/).

I'm fluent in English and Italian, and I'm currently learning German.

I built my own coding agent, [zerostack](https://github.com/gi-dellav/zerostack) (1.7k stars), for which I recieved a [1000$ OSS grant](https://rauchg-oss-grants.vercel.app/) from [Guillermo Rauch](https://rauchg.com/about).

Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/gi-dellav) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/giuseppe-della-vedova-a2890a413/). You can also subscribe to this blog via [RSS](/index.xml).
